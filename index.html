Current research

Monetary policy communication

Communication in Times of War and Political Turmoil

With Wook Sohn and Vira Vyshnevska. This project studies how the clarity and information content of turmoil-related central bank communication affect near-term exchange-rate volatility in emerging and developing economies.

View presentation slides

Global financial cycle

Central Bank Information Effects

With Cory Baird, Jonathan Benchimol, Vira Vyshnevska, and Wook Sohn. The project examines how central bank information disclosure shapes the domestic transmission of global financial shocks.

View conference poster

Research infrastructure

Monetary Policy Statement Database

A reproducible database of 6,693 statements from 51 central banks, supporting cross-country research with text analysis and large language models.
